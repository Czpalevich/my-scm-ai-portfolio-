Nine real supply chain problems I learned to solve with AI — from my Master's in Supply Chain Management at the University of Maryland's Robert H. Smith School of Business.

  • Transportation Mode Selection

    Quantitative Discovery

    2 of 6 shipping lanes were locked into Air-only routing, concentrating $19,100 of cost — nearly as much as the other four lanes combined — into a fifth of total shipments.

  • Warehouse Operations Dashboard

    Productivity Gain

    Cleaned 25 messy orders, calculated 12 KPIs, and built a live Excel dashboard in a single session — work that takes hours of manual spreadsheet cleanup otherwise.

    Productivity Gain

    Funding the cheaper bottleneck first, not the bigger one, produced 2.87 more units/hour of added throughput (59.23 vs. 56.36 units/hour, about 5% more gain) using the identical $3,500 budget, just spent in a different order.
